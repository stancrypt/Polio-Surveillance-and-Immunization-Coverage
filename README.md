<h1>Polio Surveillance and Immunization Coverage Intelligence Dashboard</h1>
<p>This project analyzes global and regional immunization coverage trends and their relationship with estimated polio cases using datasets from 
WHO and UNICEF. The objective is to identify vaccination gaps, monitor outbreak risk, and evaluate the reliability of immunization reporting across different 
regions and vaccines. Using SQL, Python, and Power BI, the project combines vaccination coverage data, surveillance indicators, and operational statistics 
to build an interactive dashboard that supports data-driven public health decision-making. The analysis highlights high-risk areas, trends in vaccination 
performance, and the potential impact of low immunization coverage on polio resurgence.</p>
